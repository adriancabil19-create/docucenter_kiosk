**Service:** DocuCenter Kiosk — Self-Service Document Processing Station
**Operator / Personal Information Controller:** Charles Adrian L. Cabil ("the Operator")
**Nature:** Undergraduate thesis prototype piloted at the University of Cebu – Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue Campus
**Contact / Data Protection point of contact:** adriancabil12@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 7 September 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how the DocuCenter Kiosk ("the Kiosk", "we", "us")
collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the printing,
scanning, photocopying, document-storage, and payment features of the Kiosk. It
is written to comply with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No.
10173, "DPA")**, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the
**National Privacy Commission (NPC)**.

Because DocuCenter is a student thesis prototype and not a registered commercial
enterprise, the scale of processing is limited to a campus pilot. We still apply
the DPA in full.

---

## 1. Summary (plain language)

- You can print, scan, and photocopy without creating an account or giving your name.
- Documents you upload or scan are held **only long enough to complete your job**, then deleted.
- Payment is handled on **PayMongo's** secure page. We never see or store your card, e-wallet, or bank credentials.
- We use **no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking**. There are no tracking cookies.
- The Kiosk keeps basic transaction records (reference number, amount, status, timestamp) for accounting and troubleshooting during the pilot.
- You may ask us to delete your files or records at any time using the contact address above.

## 2. What information we process

### 2.1 Information you provide

| Data | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Document files** (PDF, DOC/DOCX, TXT, JPG, PNG) you upload for printing or photocopying | You, via the Kiosk file picker or the phone-to-kiosk transfer feature | To render and print/copy your document |
| **Scanned images / PDFs** produced by the scanner | The Kiosk scanner, when you scan a physical document | To give you a digital copy and, if you choose, to save or transfer it |
| **File names** of the documents in a job | Derived from the files you provide | Shown to you for confirmation; stored in the print-job record |
| **Print settings** (paper size, colour mode, quality, number of copies) | You | To produce and price the job |

Document contents and file names **may contain personal data** (for example, a
résumé or an ID photocopy). We treat every uploaded or scanned file as
potentially containing personal and, in some cases, **sensitive personal
information**, and protect it accordingly.

### 2.2 Information generated by using the Kiosk

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| **Transaction record** — internal reference number, amount in PHP, status (pending/paid/failed/cancelled), service type, timestamps | Accounting reconciliation, refunds, dispute handling, thesis evaluation metrics (in aggregate) | Kept for the duration of the pilot, then deleted or anonymised |
| **Print-job record** — file names, paper size, number of copies, status, method, timestamps | Operating the printer queue, troubleshooting failed jobs | Same as above |
| **Paper-tray / device status** — sheet counts, low-paper alerts | Keeping the Kiosk stocked and working | Rolling operational data |
| **Activity logs** — system events and staff actions in the admin console | Security, accountability, debugging | Duration of the pilot |

The Kiosk database does **not** store your name, email address, phone number, or
payer identity.

### 2.3 Information handled by PayMongo (payment processor)

When you pay, the Kiosk shows a PayMongo checkout (QR code or link). Your
payment is completed on **PayMongo's** systems. Any name, email, phone number,
card number, e-wallet account, or bank details you enter there are collected and
processed by **PayMongo Philippines, Inc.** as a separate personal information
controller under its own privacy policy (<https://www.paymongo.com/privacy>).
The Kiosk receives back only a payment reference and a paid/failed status.

### 2.4 Admin console (staff only)

The admin console used by pilot staff processes a **staff username, password
(stored hashed), and a session cookie**. It is not accessible to Kiosk users.

## 3. How we use information (purposes and lawful basis)

Under the DPA we rely on the following criteria for lawful processing:

| Purpose | Lawful basis (DPA §12 / §13) |
|---|---|
| Print, copy, scan, store, or transfer the document you asked us to process | Necessary for a transaction/service you requested; and your **consent** given at the Kiosk |
| Take payment and match it to your job | Necessary for the requested transaction |
| Issue an on-screen/printed acknowledgement or receipt of payment | Legitimate interest in honest record-keeping; compliance with tax/consumer rules if the pilot becomes commercial |
| Handle refunds, failed jobs, and complaints | Necessary for the transaction; legitimate interest |
| Keep the Kiosk secure and diagnose faults (logs) | Legitimate interest in security and reliability, balanced against your rights |
| Report **aggregated, non-identifying** usage statistics in the thesis | Legitimate interest / research; no personal data is included |

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, automated
decision-making with legal effects, or sale to third parties.

Processing of **sensitive personal information** (which a scanned ID or medical
document may contain) is done **only with your consent** and only to deliver the
job you requested.

## 4. Retention and deletion

| Item | Retention rule |
|---|---|
| Files uploaded for printing/photocopying | Deleted after the job is completed or abandoned. Staff also run a scheduled purge; target maximum retention **24 hours**. You may delete a file yourself from the **Storage** screen. |
| Scanned images before you save/transfer them | Working copies are deleted automatically once the scan is delivered or the session ends. |
| Phone ⇄ Kiosk transfer sessions | Expire and are wiped automatically **30 minutes** after creation. |
| Transaction and print-job records | Retained for the pilot period for reconciliation, then deleted or anonymised. No document contents are kept — only metadata described in §2.2. |
| Activity logs | Retained for the pilot period, then deleted. |

If you want your files or records deleted sooner, contact
adriancabil12@gmail.com with the reference number shown on your receipt.

> **Pilot note:** automatic purging of the local document store is being
> enforced by a scheduled task. Until it is fully in place, staff perform the
> purge manually at least daily. See `docs/PH_COMPLIANCE.md`.

## 5. Disclosure and sharing

We share personal data only in these situations:

- **PayMongo Philippines, Inc.** — to process your payment (see §2.3).
- **The University of Cebu**, as host of the pilot — only aggregated,
  non-identifying statistics, and incident reports that do not include document
  contents.
- **When required by law** — e.g., a lawful order from a court or the NPC.
- **To protect rights and safety** — e.g., investigating misuse of the scanner
  for unlawful content.

We do **not** transfer personal data outside the Philippines except insofar as
PayMongo or the transfer-relay hosting provider (Railway) operate infrastructure
abroad; those providers are bound by their own contractual and legal safeguards.
The Kiosk itself and its local document store run on-premises on campus.

## 6. Security measures

- Documents are processed on the local Kiosk machine and a campus-controlled
  backend; the local store is not exposed to the public internet.
- Transfer-relay sessions are short-lived (30 minutes) and the files are deleted
  on expiry.
- The admin console requires authentication, stores passwords hashed, uses
  signed session cookies, and restricts cross-origin access.
- Payment credentials never touch the Kiosk — they are entered on PayMongo.
- Access to the Kiosk hardware and admin console is limited to authorised pilot
  staff.
- We keep the number of data fields collected to the minimum needed
  (data minimisation).

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely
to cause real risk to you, we will notify the **NPC and affected individuals
within 72 hours** of knowledge of the breach, as required by NPC Circular
16-03.

## 7. Your rights as a data subject

Under the DPA you have the right to:

1. **Be informed** — this policy, plus the short notice shown at the Kiosk.
2. **Access** — ask what data about you we hold.
3. **Object** — to processing, including withdrawing consent (this may mean we
   cannot complete your job).
4. **Rectification** — correct inaccurate records.
5. **Erasure or blocking** — ask us to delete or suspend processing of your data.
6. **Data portability** — get an electronic copy of data you provided.
7. **Damages** — be indemnified for damage from unlawful processing.
8. **File a complaint** with the National Privacy Commission.

To exercise any right, email **adriancabil12@gmail.com**. We will respond
within a reasonable period, ordinarily within **15 working days**. There is no
charge for a reasonable request.

**National Privacy Commission** — 5th Floor, Philippine International Convention
Center, Vicente Sotto St., Pasay City · <https://privacy.gov.ph> ·
complaints@privacy.gov.ph

## 8. Children

The Kiosk is intended for use by university students, faculty, and staff. Some
users may be minors. We do not knowingly collect more personal data than needed
to process a document job, and we do not build profiles of any user. A parent or
guardian may contact us to have a minor's records deleted.

## 9. Cookies and local storage

The Kiosk app and the staff admin console use **only strictly necessary local
storage** (an admin session cookie, and a local cache of your current job on the
Kiosk). There are **no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking
cookies**, so no cookie-consent banner is required. See
[`cookie-policy.md`](cookie-policy.md) for detail.
