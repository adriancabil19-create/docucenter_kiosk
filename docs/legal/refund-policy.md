This Refund Policy forms part of the [Terms &amp; Conditions](terms-and-conditions.md).
It is consistent with the **Consumer Act of the Philippines (RA 7394)**, which
entitles consumers to a remedy when a service is not rendered with due skill or
as described.

---

## 1. When you are entitled to a refund

You are entitled to a **full refund** (or, at your choice, a free re-run of the
job) if you paid and the Kiosk failed to deliver the service through no fault of
yours, including:

- Printer jam, out-of-paper, out-of-ink, or hardware failure during your job;
- Power interruption or software crash before your job completed;
- The Kiosk charged you but produced **no output** or **materially defective
  output** (e.g., blank pages, wrong file printed by the system, severe quality
  defect not caused by your source file);
- You were charged **more than the amount shown** on the confirmation screen;
- **Duplicate charge** for a single job.

---

## 2. When a refund is not available

- The output matches your uploaded file and chosen settings, but you are
  unhappy with your **own** document's layout, content, or resolution.
- You selected the wrong settings (e.g., colour vs. black &amp; white, wrong paper
  size) and the Kiosk printed exactly what you selected. *Staff may still grant
  a goodwill re-run or partial refund at their discretion during the pilot.*
- Scanning or photocopying was completed and delivered, but you no longer want
  it.
- The charge was made on **PayMongo's** side but never reached the Kiosk as
  "paid"; in that case PayMongo either did not capture the funds (no refund
  needed) or will reverse an uncaptured authorisation automatically. Contact us
  if a charge appears on your statement without a completed job.

---

## 3. How to request a refund

1. At the Kiosk, note the **reference number** on the on-screen or printed
   acknowledgement.
2. Email **adriancabil12@gmail.com** with:
   - the reference number,
   - the date and approximate time,
   - a short description of what went wrong,
   - the amount charged.
3. If pilot staff are present at the Kiosk, you may raise it with them
   immediately; they will record the claim.

Please contact us **within 7 days** of the transaction so we can match it to
Kiosk logs.

---

## 4. How refunds are paid and how long they take

- Approved refunds are returned **to the original payment method** through
  PayMongo.
- We aim to submit the refund within **3 working days** of approving your claim.
- PayMongo and your bank or e-wallet provider then take additional time to post
  the funds — typically a few working days, occasionally longer for card
  refunds.
- We do not charge a fee to process a valid refund.

---

## 5. Failed or uncertain payments

If the payment screen timed out or errored, the job is automatically cancelled
and, in the normal case, **no charge is finalised**. If you still see a pending
or completed charge with no output, treat it as §1 and contact us.

---

## 6. Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit your rights under the Consumer Act (RA 7394) or other
Philippine law. If we cannot resolve your claim, you may refer it to the
**Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)** consumer protection service.
