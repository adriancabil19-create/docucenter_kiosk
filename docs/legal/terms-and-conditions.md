## 1. What the Kiosk does

The Kiosk lets you, on a self-service basis:

- **Print** documents you upload or that you transfer from your phone;
- **Scan** physical documents into digital files;
- **Photocopy** physical documents;
- **Store** documents temporarily on the Kiosk for a later job;
- **Pay** for paid services through PayMongo.

The Kiosk is a **thesis prototype**. It is provided for evaluation during a
campus pilot and may be unavailable, changed, or withdrawn at any time.
