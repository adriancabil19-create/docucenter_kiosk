# Data-Processing Consent Notice (shown at the Kiosk)

**Service:** DocuCenter Kiosk
**Operator:** Charles Adrian L. Cabil — adriancabil12@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 7 September 2026
**Version:** 1.0

This is the short, just-in-time notice presented to users at the Kiosk before a
paid job and before a document is uploaded, scanned, or transferred. It
implements the "right to be informed" and the consent requirement of the **Data
Privacy Act (RA 10173)**. The full detail is in the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

---

## Short notice (screen text)

> **Before you continue**
>
> To do this job, the DocuCenter Kiosk will briefly process the file(s) you
> provide or scan. Files are used only to complete your job and are deleted
> afterwards (within 24 hours at the latest). We keep a payment record
> (reference number, amount, status, time) — **not** your name or your
> document's contents.
>
> Payment is handled by **PayMongo** on its own secure page; we never see your
> card or e-wallet details.
>
> We use **no tracking and no analytics**.
>
> By ticking the box below you confirm you have read the
> **Terms &amp; Conditions**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Refund Policy**, that you
> are allowed to copy the document(s) you are submitting, and that you consent
> to this processing.
>
> ☐ I have read and agree to the Terms &amp; Conditions, Privacy Policy, and
> Refund Policy.
>

## Withdrawing consent

You can stop at any time before payment by pressing **Cancel**; your files are
discarded. After a job, you can ask us to delete any remaining records by
emailing **adriancabil12@gmail.com** with your reference number.
